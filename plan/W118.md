## Monday — O5 part 1: the protocol-mechanics analysis (does "dropped-before-slot" happen under 2-chain + TC?)

- [ ] [Venue] **Decision-analysis (the gate, part 1)**: `crates/consensus-vsr/notes/118mon_o5_mechanics.md` — settle, on **protocol mechanics**, whether a leader's proposal can be **dropped before ever occupying a committed log position** under the chosen pipelined-HotStuff (Jolteon 2-chain + Timeout-Certificate) pacemaker. **Do NOT anchor on W91's "current belief" (derive-from-`drain_committed`).** Weigh the case the project itself raised. (~3.5 hrs)

  - 🔧 **Why this matters**: W91's abandonment mechanism derives "my proposal died" from the ledger observing its proposed `OpNumber` filled by a *different* op in `drain_committed`. That works for VSR (dense op-numbers assigned at propose time; a view-change re-fills position *n*). The open threat: BFT may orphan a proposal with **no fill event to observe**. If that case is real, derivation is blind to it → a pending op hangs forever (a liveness/correctness bug that type-checks, passes `bft_stub_compiles` AND `test_routine_rotation_does_not_drain_pending`, and surfaces only at the W135 swap). This is precisely the failure mode the seam exists to prevent — so it must be settled on mechanics, now.

  - *Mental model*: VSR has a **dense linear log**; every op-number is a position that *will* be filled by exactly one op (the view-change re-proposes the uncommitted suffix). BFT has a **block tree**; the committed chain is one path through it, and non-committed branches are **pruned, not superseded**. "Position" in BFT is a tree node (parent-hash + view), not a dense slot — and a pruned branch leaves no observable fill at any committed position.

  - **The analysis to perform** — answer each in the note with protocol-level justification:
    - A1. **Jolteon block lifecycle.** The leader of view *v* proposes block `B_v` extending the **high-QC** (highest QC it knows). Votes form `QC(B_v)`; the 2-chain rule commits `B_v` when `QC(B_v)` exists *and* `QC(B′)` exists where `B′` is `B_v`'s direct child **and `B′.round == B_v.round + 1` (consecutive rounds)** — ancestors commit transitively. A direct-child QC certified across a TC gap is exactly the unsafe case the published rule excludes. Write the lifecycle and the three points where `B_v` can die: (i) leader never assembles `QC(B_v)` (Byzantine/withholding); (ii) pacemaker timeout → TC → view *v+1*; (iii) the *v+1* leader extends the high-QC, which (since `B_v` got no QC) does **not** include `B_v`.
    - A2. **The dropped-before-slot trace (the load-bearing one).** Write the exact sequence: leader *v* proposes `B_v` (ledger called `propose(op)`); leader *v* is slow; replicas time out → TC → view *v+1*; leader *v+1* extends high-QC = `QC(parent of B_v)`, proposing `B_{v+1}` as a **sibling** of `B_v`; the committed chain advances through `B_{v+1}, B_{v+2}, …` and **never includes `B_v`, and never places any block "at `B_v`'s position"** (B_v was a tree branch, now pruned). Conclude: there is **no fill event** at a committed `OpNumber` the proposer can watch — `B_v`'s op simply never appears in `drain_committed`.
    - A3. **Why VSR doesn't have this.** Contrast: VSR assigns dense op-number *n* at propose; on view-change the new leader re-proposes the uncommitted suffix, so op-number *n* IS filled (by the same or a different op) and the supersession is observable. BFT (with O4 densification: dense `OpNumber` assigned at **commit**) never assigns `B_v` a committed position to be superseded at.
    - A4. **The deeper finding (does `propose -> OpNumber` even survive?).** Under O4 densification the committed `OpNumber` is assigned at **commit time** and may never exist for an orphaned proposal. So `propose(op) -> Result<OpNumber, NotLeader>` returning a dense `OpNumber` **synchronously** is itself a VSR-shaped assumption: BFT cannot honestly return a committed position at propose time. The W91 ledger records that returned number and watches it — but under BFT it was never a real committed position. State whether this means the abandonment fix is *coupled* to a `propose` return-type change (it is — see Tuesday).
    - A5. **Falsification attempt (steelman the "no surface needed" side).** Try hard to make derivation work: could the ledger infer orphaning from `commit_point()` advancing past the *height* `B_v` was proposed at? Show why this fails: (i) it requires exposing BFT **height** (not dense `OpNumber`) — leaking BFT semantics into the call site, the exact thing the seam bans; (ii) `drain_committed` delivers dense `OpNumber`s, not heights; (iii) even with height, the mapping height→committed-OpNumber is non-trivial under pruning. Conclude the steelman fails.
  - 🎯 **Hardened acceptance**: the note contains the A2 trace at message granularity (proposal / votes / timeout / TC / new-view proposal), the A3 VSR contrast, and an explicit verdict on A4/A5. The verdict must be one of: **(YES, real → needs surface)** or **(NO, every orphan manifests as an observable fill → derivation suffices, prove it)** — with the proof, not a hand-wave. *Per the mechanics above, the honest verdict is YES; if your analysis reaches NO, the A5 steelman must be airtight or you've papered over the leak.*
  - *Pre-mortem (the leak we're hunting)*: a reviewer who anchors on W91's "current belief" rubber-stamps "derivation suffices," W119 builds on the frozen trait, and at W135 the first orphaned-before-slot proposal hangs a client forever in a Bet #1 work-trial demo. The whole point of W118-as-a-gate is to catch this on a whiteboard, not in a demo.

## Tuesday — O5 RESOLUTION: decision + exact signature addition + W91 amendment list + HALT

- [ ] [Venue] **The O5 resolution** (the gate, part 2): `crates/consensus-vsr/notes/118tue_o5_resolution.md`. (~3 hrs)

  **§ Decision (with protocol justification):** **O5 resolves to "the outer trait NEEDS a dedicated abandonment surface."**
  Protocol-level justification (from Monday A2/A4): under Jolteon 2-chain + TC, a proposal can be **orphaned before
  occupying any committed position** (pacemaker view-change extends the high-QC, pruning the proposal's branch). No fill
  event exists at a committed `OpNumber` for the proposer to observe, so the W91 derive-from-`drain_committed` mechanism
  is **structurally blind** to it. Furthermore, `propose -> OpNumber` is itself VSR-shaped: under O4 densification the
  committed `OpNumber` is assigned at commit and may never exist. **Therefore the abandonment fix is coupled to a
  `propose` return-type change.** This is the **one sanctioned outer-trait addition** the W91 seam reserved for W118.

  **§ Exact signature addition** (the minimal, frozen-after-this surface):
  ```rust
  // NEW opaque proposal token — returned at propose, correlates commit/abandon back to the caller.
  #[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq)] pub struct ProposalHandle(u64);

  pub trait ConsensusBackbone {
      type Op;
      type Commit<'a>: ConsensusCommit<Op = Self::Op> where Self: 'a;
      // CHANGED: returns an opaque handle, NOT an OpNumber (BFT can't know the committed position at propose time).
      fn propose(&mut self, op: Self::Op) -> Result<ProposalHandle, NotLeader>;
      fn drain_committed(&mut self, sink: &mut dyn FnMut(Self::Commit<'_>));
      // NEW: the dedicated abandonment surface — reports proposals that will never commit (orphaned / superseded).
      fn drain_abandoned(&mut self, sink: &mut dyn FnMut(ProposalHandle));
      fn commit_point(&self) -> OpNumber;
      fn applied_point(&self) -> OpNumber;
      fn role(&self) -> Role;
  }
  pub trait ConsensusCommit {
      type Op;
      fn op_number(&self) -> OpNumber;
      fn op(&self) -> &Self::Op;
      // NEW: correlate a commit back to the local proposal (Some on the proposer; None on followers / replay).
      fn proposal_handle(&self) -> Option<ProposalHandle>;
  }
  ```
  **Behavioral contract for the new surface** (must hold for BOTH VSR and BFT):
  - `propose` returns a `ProposalHandle` immediately; the handle is the caller's correlation key, **not** a commit promise and **not** a position.
  - Every proposal terminates **exactly once**: either it appears in `drain_committed` with `proposal_handle() == Some(h)`, **or** it appears in `drain_abandoned(h)` — never both, never neither (liveness: the backbone guarantees one terminal event per handle).
  - `drain_abandoned` covers BOTH BFT orphaned-before-slot **and** VSR view-change supersession (VSR maps both to the same surface — so VSR's ledger uses the identical handle-based path; no VSR-special-case).
  - `OpNumber` is now **commit-time only** — it appears only on a `Commit`, never from `propose`. (This is what makes O4 densification clean: `propose` no longer pretends to know the dense position.)

  **§ Why this is minimal (not scope creep):** two coupled additions (`drain_abandoned` + `propose`→`ProposalHandle` + `Commit::proposal_handle`), all serving the single concern "tell the caller a proposal died." No quorum/auth/view/equivocation vocabulary leaks; the four inner seams are untouched; the `dyn`-at-boundary + monomorphized-inner-seams structure is unchanged.

## Wednesday — O1: authentication-cost strategy (impl-only, but it sizes X)

- [ ] [Venue] **Decision doc**: `crates/consensus-vsr/notes/118wed_o1_auth.md` — pick the message-auth strategy for the N=4 MVP and state its effect on the latency target X. (~3 hrs)

  - *The problem*: `SignedPeers::verify` (the BFT `Authenticator` impl) costs ~20–50 µs/msg with per-message ed25519, on the vote/proposal hot path. The candidates: (a) BLS signature aggregation (one verify per QC, not per vote); (b) ed25519 with batch-verification on QC formation; (c) hardware offload.
  - **Decision (plan of record)**: **ed25519 with batch-verification on the QC-formation path** for the N=4 MVP; **BLS aggregation deferred to the large-N production phase** behind the *same* `Authenticator` seam.
  - **Justification (the N=4 nuance)**: at N=4, a QC needs 2f+1 = 3 votes. Batch-verifying 3 ed25519 signatures (`ed25519-dalek` batch API) ≈ ~50–80 µs total, vs ~120–150 µs unbatched, vs a **BLS aggregate verify ≈ ~1–2 ms** (a single pairing check — *slower* at small N because the pairing dominates). **BLS wins only at large N** (one aggregate verify vs N individual), which is the post-MVP large-validator-set trajectory — and because auth is already the `Authenticator` seam, swapping ed25519→BLS later is a seam-impl change, **no design debt**. So: simplest-that-meets-X now, BLS as a known seam-swap later.
  - **Effect on X (the number)**: the 2-chain commit path touches ~2 QCs; QC verify ≈ ~50–80 µs each (batched) ⇒ ~120–160 µs of signature verification per committed batch. That fits comfortably under the X locked Thursday (p99 ≤ 2 ms) with the rest of the budget going to round-trips.
  - **O1 status**: confirmed **impl-only** — the `Authenticator` seam already carries `verify` on the recv path (W91); this decision picks the *impl*, changes no interface. The ed25519→BLS swap is a future seam-impl change.
  - 🎯 **Hardened acceptance**: the doc names the chosen impl, the N=4 batch-verify cost, the BLS-at-large-N deferral with its trigger (validator set > ~16, where aggregate verify beats N individual), and the contribution to X. A doc that picks BLS for N=4 "because BFT = BLS" without the small-N pairing-cost analysis is RED.

      | Replica | in Q1? | in Q2? | in Q1∩Q2? | may be Byzantine (f=1)? |
      |---|---|---|---|---|
      | R0 | ✓ | — | — | at most one of the four |
      | R1 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | " |
      | R2 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | " |
      | R3 | — | ✓ | — | " |

## Friday — Byzantine scenario set for the W129–W134 VOPR + O3/O4/O6 carry-forward

- [ ] [Venue] **Decision doc**: `crates/consensus-vsr/notes/118fri_byzantine_scenarios.md` — fix the exact Byzantine fault set the W129–W134 cluster-VOPR must explore (so VOPR coverage is a lock, not a vibe). (~2.5 hrs)
  - **Scenario set (each: the fault, the invariant it must not break, the StateChecker assertion):**
    1. **Equivocation** — a leader double-proposes two different blocks at the same height/view. Invariant: safety (no two conflicting blocks commit). Assertion: `DetectEquivocation` emits `Evidence`; no conflicting commit.
    2. **Vote-withholding** *(pacemaker-introduced)* — leader/replica stays silent. Invariant: liveness via pacemaker. Assertion: TC forms, view advances, progress resumes; pending proposals of the silent leader hit **`drain_abandoned`** (the O5 surface — this scenario directly exercises the amendment).
    3. **Leader-equivocation / conflicting QCs** — a Byzantine leader tries to form two QCs on conflicting branches. Invariant: quorum intersection (2f+1 of 3f+1 share an honest node). Assertion: at most one QC per **view/round** (quorum intersection — each replica votes once per round); the honest overlap prevents the second. *Note: two QCs at the same HEIGHT in different rounds is a legal schedule (the O5 orphan path produces it); conflicting COMMITS are prevented by the 2-chain rule — that is scenario #4's assertion, kept distinct.*
    4. **Safety-attack (conflicting commit attempt)** — adversary schedules messages to try to commit two conflicting blocks. Invariant: 2-chain safety. Assertion: StateChecker linearizability holds; no fork.
    5. **Partition + Byzantine combined** — network partition with a Byzantine node in the majority side. Invariant: safety always; liveness when partition heals. Assertion: no bad commit during partition; convergence after.
    6. **Stale-high-QC proposal** *(pacemaker-introduced)* — a Byzantine new-view leader proposes extending an old QC to orphan recent blocks. Invariant: replicas reject proposals not extending the highest known QC. Assertion: the stale proposal gets no quorum.
    7. **Orphaned-proposal-before-slot** *(the O5 case — REQUIRED)* — force a proposal to be orphaned by a pacemaker view-change without supersession. Assertion: **`drain_abandoned` fires for exactly that proposal's handle, exactly once**; the proposer's client gets `Abandoned`, not a hang. *This scenario is the executable proof that the O5 amendment closed the leak.*
    8. **OpNumber contiguity under Byzantine view-changes** *(O4)* — across Byzantine-leader view changes, assert committed `OpNumber`s stay dense + contiguous (densification holds). Assertion: no gaps/dupes in the committed `OpNumber` sequence.
  - Mark (2), (6), (7) as **pacemaker-introduced** (they exist only because BFT has a pacemaker/view-synchronizer — VSR's VOPR didn't need them).
