  #### Phase 1 — Problem, requirements, design (~60 min)

  - **The problem this primitive solves**: every other queue in the workspace eventually wants "bounded,
    lock-free, MPMC, low-allocation, scales to 16+ producers/consumers". Vyukov's per-slot seq design is the
    canonical answer; the trap is composing it correctly with CachePadded (separate lines), Backoff
    (idle CPU), and Parker (wake on empty/full).

  - **Requirements** — write in `crates/concurrent/notes/11_mpmc_design.md`:
    - R1. Power-of-2 capacity; mask index via `idx & (cap-1)`, never `%`.
    - R2. Per-slot `seq: AtomicUsize`. Producer claims when `seq == lap*cap + idx`; releases with `Release`.

    - R3. `head` and `tail` cursors each `CachePadded<AtomicUsize>` (W4 Mon). Struct `#[repr(C)]`.
    - R4. Producer/consumer wait via Backoff ladder (W4 Tue) → Parker (W4 Thu). No raw spin.
    - R5. **Latency thresholds**: send p50 ≤30ns, p99 ≤100ns, p99.9 ≤500ns uncontended.
    - R6. **Scale curve**: at N=16, aggregate throughput ≥10× N=1.
    - R7. **Idle CPU**: 0 contention for 60s → <5% CPU.
    - R8. Loom-green at cap=2 (forces wraparound), 2P+2C, 8 ops.
    - R9. Zero alloc steady-state (dhat-verified). Construction + cold Parker may alloc.

    - D3. Why CachePadded on head+tail? Walk through false-sharing scenario (producer touches tail,

    - D4. Why Backoff ladder not raw spin? Walk through 16-producer contention; raw spin = 100% CPU + MESI

  #### Phase 3 — Make the tests pass, one failure at a time (~120 min)

  - **When `mpmc_loom_2p_2c_cap2_wraparound` reports "slot N holds two values" within seconds:**
    - Symptom: loom finds a schedule where two producers wrote to the same slot.
    - Why: capacity not power-of-2 OR you used `%` instead of mask; aliasing introduced.
    - Fix: assert `cap.is_power_of_two()` in `new()`; use `idx & (cap - 1)` mask everywhere.
    - Muscle: power-of-2 + mask is the safe index; modulo allows aliasing under non-pow2. Reapplies at:
      W26 SegQueue, W77 aeron term buffer.

  - **When `mpmc_loom_2p_2c_cap2_wraparound` reports "consumer observed uninit slot data" on aarch:**
    - Symptom: consumer sees seq bumped but slot still has old/garbage data.
    - Why: seq store with Relaxed; aarch64 reorders the seq store before the data write.
    - Fix: producer's seq-release uses `Release`; consumer's seq-load uses `Acquire`. Pair carries the
      data write.
    - Muscle: per-slot seq is a lock — Release on producer-bump pairs with Acquire on consumer-check.
      Reapplies at: W26 SegQueue, W65 Disruptor, W77 aeron.

  - **When `mpmc_head_tail_separate_cache_lines` reports offset_of delta = 8 (same line):**
    - Symptom: head + tail on same 64B line. Producer touches tail, consumer touches head, false-share.
    - Why: declared `struct { head: AtomicUsize, tail: AtomicUsize }` without CachePadded or repr(C)
      reordered fields.
    - Fix: `#[repr(C)] struct BoundedRing { head: CachePadded<_>, tail: CachePadded<_>, buf: … }`.
      `offset_of!` static assert delta >= 128.
    - Cost avoided: ~100ns per contended op — the cross-core cache-line bounce costs ~30 cycles of MESI
      invalidation (RFO + writeback) on *every* `send`/`recv` under contention, vs ~20ns uncontended; that is
      a 5× per-op regression that silently disqualifies the crate from its SLA **(bar (c) violation)**.
    - Muscle: producer + consumer cursors on a lock-free queue MUST be in separate cache lines.
      Reapplies at: W26 SegQueue head/tail, W65 Disruptor claim/cursor, W74 raft term+index, W77 aeron
      term rotation counter pair.

  - **When `mpmc_recv_on_empty_blocks_via_parker` shows 100% CPU:**
    - Symptom: consumer burns a core.
    - Why: bare while-loop without Backoff or Parker.
    - Fix: Backoff ladder; after N spins, Parker::park. Sender calls `unpark` after success.
    - Cost avoided: 1 full core pinned at 100% spin → <5% CPU when idle, and zero allocations on the park path
      (cold Parker may alloc once). On a thread-per-core HFT runtime, a spinning idle consumer steals a core
      that should be running another shard **(bar (c) violation)**.
    - Muscle: every blocking primitive eventually parks; Backoff is the ladder. Reapplies at: W26 WAL
      group-commit ack, W42 LockManager waiter wake.

  - **When `mpmc_scale_curve_16x_at_n16` shows <5× at N=16:**
    - Symptom: sublinear scaling; sub-cliff.
    - Why: either head/tail on same line (CachePadded missing), or producers contend on a single CAS
      that should be per-slot.
    - Fix: re-verify CachePadded + per-slot seq; profile under criterion's multi-thread harness; if
      still sub-cliff, head-counter contention dominates and you need producer-sharded heads (W14
      drill).
    - Cost avoided: aggregate throughput collapse from ~10×→<5× at N=16 — i.e. losing ~25M+ ops/sec of the
      target on a 16-core box; the single hot cursor line costs ~25-30 cycles of CAS-retry per contended
      claim **(bar (c) violation)**.
    - Muscle: scaling cliffs ALWAYS point at a single hot cache line. Reapplies at: W42 next_id, W74
      raft batching.

  - **(EXPERT) When `mpmc_zero_alloc_steady_state` reports allocations after warm-up:**
    - Symptom: dhat shows N>0 allocations across 10k steady-state send/recv ops.
    - Why: a `Box`/`Vec`/`Arc` on the hot path — typically boxing the value into the slot, or a per-`send`
      `Parker` allocation instead of one cold parker reused across parks.
    - Fix: store `T` inline in the slot (`MaybeUninit<T>`), construct + cold-Parker-init outside steady state.
    - Cost avoided: ~50ns malloc + lock/arena contention per op × 10k ops = ~500µs of pure allocator time per
      burst, plus unbounded tail latency from allocator locks — exactly 1 alloc/op too many on a zero-alloc
      hot path **(bar (c) violation)**.
    - Muscle: the steady-state hot path of a bar (c) primitive allocates ZERO — push the allocation to
      construction. Reapplies at: W14 bufpool lease, W26 wal append, W76 aeron publication claim.

    practical, the per-slot-seq lock-free design loses to lock-free + wait-free hybrids. Migration:
    abstract the Channel trait; LCRQ as a behind-feature impl. Trigger: ≥256 producers in a real
