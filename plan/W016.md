So correct pin ≈ ~13ns vs ~6ns naive. **Cliff: the naive 6ns version is a UAF, not a slow version — the 7ns fence is the price of not segfaulting under load.** At >50M pins/s the fence is ~0.35 CPU-seconds/s — visible but mandatory.
