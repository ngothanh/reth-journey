This is an execution week, not a build week. The artifact each day produces is a **structured debrief** captured the same evening, while memory is fresh — debriefs are the only durable output of an interview and they drive W129's prep deltas and W131's offer-decision matrix. Treat every interview as a source of signal you must record, not a pass/fail event you forget. Day estimates below cover the debrief + targeted recovery prep; the interview itself is a full external day and is not double-counted in the hour budget.

**Monday — Final round: firm 1 (4-5 interviewers, full day: coding, systems design, behavioral, matching-engine deep-dive)**

- [ ] Pre-flight: re-read `interview_prep/firm1_dossier.md` (role, interviewers, known stack, the 2-3 questions you will ask them). (~30 min)
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: dossier names every scheduled interviewer + their likely focus area, and lists ≥3 questions you will ask that are specific to firm 1's stack (not generic "what's the culture") — each question tied to something you can only know from W123-W124 recon or their public engineering writing.
  - *Mental model*: a final round is bidirectional — you are evaluating fit as hard as they evaluate you, and specific questions are the cheapest, highest-signal way to show you did the homework that 90% of candidates skip. The dossier converts pre-interview adrenaline into a checklist so prep is a recall, not a scramble.
  - Output: `interview_prep/firm1_dossier.md` updated with interviewer list + ≥3 stack-specific questions; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the pre-flight commit.
- [ ] Same-evening debrief: write `interview_debriefs/firm1_final.md` while memory is fresh. (~45 min)
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: debrief covers all 4 segments (coding / systems design / behavioral / matching-engine deep-dive) with, per segment: the exact problem/prompt, what you answered, where you stumbled, and one concrete prep delta for W129. The matching-engine segment records which design decisions (single-writer ring, deterministic replay, back-pressure) you defended well vs. got pushed on.
  - *Mental model*: the deep-dive into matching-engine is where 119 weeks of work pays off or doesn't — interviewers probe for whether you actually understand the single-writer principle and back-pressure-as-correctness, or just recite them. Recording where you got pushed turns a fuzzy "I think it went okay" into a precise list of what to tighten before firm 2 and firm 3.
  - Output: `interview_debriefs/firm1_final.md` exists with 4 segment sections + a `## Prep deltas for W129` list of ≥2 concrete items; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the Monday debrief commit.

**Tuesday — Recover + targeted patch from firm 1 deltas**

- [ ] Triage Monday's prep deltas: pick the 1-2 highest-leverage items and do a focused 60-min repair (re-derive the answer you fumbled, re-draw the design you got pushed on). (~75 min)
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: each chosen delta from `firm1_final.md` is closed with a written corrected answer or re-drawn diagram in `interview_prep/patches.md`, dated and labeled with its source firm — not just "reviewed" but a concrete artifact you could hand to the next interviewer.
  - *Mental model*: recovery days are where the interview cycle compounds — the same questions recur across firms (especially the matching-engine deep-dive), so a fumble at firm 1 patched Tuesday becomes a strength at firm 2 Wednesday. Skipping the patch wastes the most expensive signal you'll get all week.
  - Output: `interview_prep/patches.md` has ≥1 dated, sourced patch entry closing a Monday delta; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the Tuesday patch commit.

**Wednesday — Final round: firm 2 (full day)**

- [ ] Pre-flight: re-read `interview_prep/firm2_dossier.md` + skim `interview_prep/patches.md` so Monday's fumbles don't repeat. (~30 min)
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: firm 2 dossier meets the same bar as firm 1's (interviewers + ≥3 stack-specific questions), AND you have explicitly noted which Tuesday patches apply to firm 2's likely question set.
  - *Mental model*: firm 2 should be measurably better than firm 1 because you arrive with a debrief and a patch list — the cycle is a learning loop, and the pre-flight is where last round's lesson gets loaded before it can be repeated.
  - Output: `interview_prep/firm2_dossier.md` updated to the same bar; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the pre-flight commit.
- [ ] Same-evening debrief: write `interview_debriefs/firm2_final.md`. (~45 min)
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: same 4-segment structure as firm 1, PLUS a one-line comparison note vs. firm 1 (which felt stronger on team, comp signal, technical depth, remote-from-Vietnam friendliness) — the first input to the W131 offer-decision matrix.
  - *Mental model*: comparison data is only capturable while both rounds are fresh; by W131 the firms blur together. Logging the head-to-head impression now is what makes the offer decision evidence-based rather than recency-biased.
  - Output: `interview_debriefs/firm2_final.md` exists with 4 segments + `## Prep deltas for W129` + a `## vs firm 1` line; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the Wednesday debrief commit.

**Thursday — Recover + targeted patch from firm 2 deltas**

- [ ] Triage Wednesday's deltas and repair the top 1-2 before firm 3. (~75 min)

  - *Mental model*: a fumble that appears at two independent firms is a real gap in your model, not bad luck — tagging recurrence forces you to fix the root cause (e.g. you can't crisply justify a design tradeoff) rather than memorizing one answer.
  - Output: `interview_prep/patches.md` has the Thursday patch entries with any `[recurring]` tags; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the Thursday patch commit.

  - Output: `interview_debriefs/decision_matrix.md` exists with a 3-firm × ≥6-dimension scored table, per-cell evidence citations, a current-leader pick, and a missing-info line; `git log -1 --oneline` shows the Saturday synthesis commit.
