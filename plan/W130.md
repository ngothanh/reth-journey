This is a career/offer-wait week, not a Build week. There is no crate, no upstream mirror, and no engineered trap-test arc — manufacturing one would be padding. The week's real job is a decision-quality job: keep Tier-A processes warm without going needy, open a Tier-B safety net so you never negotiate from zero leverage, and pre-commit your decision rubric BEFORE any offer lands so the W131 decision is made by your sober self, not your offer-day adrenaline. Every item below carries an explicit artifact file + an Output line that proves it done; the Saturday decision-criteria item carries a hardened-acceptance pass criterion and a mental model of why it exists now.

**Monday — Tier-B firm A application**

- [ ] Artifact: append to `progress.md` under a `## Tier-B applications` heading (private). If by W130 there is no Tier-A offer in hand, activate Tier-B firm A: submit the application, log {firm, role, date sent, referral channel if any, expected comp band}. Tier-B compensation is materially below destination-tier but still well above home — it is the floor option that guarantees you walk away from W131 with at least one path, not zero. Record the gate decision at the top: `Tier-A offer in hand: yes/no` — if yes, write the one-line reason you are still opening Tier-B as a negotiation anchor (or skip and say so). (~30 min)
  - *Mental model*: a candidate with no alternative cannot negotiate — every recruiter can smell it. Opening Tier-B turns "please hire me" into "I'm choosing between options," which is the single biggest lever on the W131 outcome. The floor option also caps your downside risk: the README risk register flags day-job-exit-without-grace, and a signed Tier-B offer makes the W132 resignation reversible-in-spirit.
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: `progress.md` opens the Tier-B section with the `Tier-A offer in hand: yes/no` gate decision + reason; firm A logged with {role, date sent, comp band, channel}; if the gate said "skip," the skip reason is written, not blank.

**Saturday — Decision-criteria pre-write**

- [ ] Artifact: `progress.md` under a `## Offer decision criteria (pre-written W130)` heading (private). BEFORE any offer lands, write the rubric you will score W131 offers against. Capture each dimension with an explicit weight and a concrete pass-bar, not a vibe: (1) compensation — rank vs your recorded benchmark + your walk-away floor number; (2) team/manager fit — the specific signals from interviews that read green/red; (3) work-from-home / remote-from-Vietnam flexibility — fully-remote vs relocation-required, a hard requirement vs nice-to-have; (4) relocation logistics + visa tractability — cross-referenced against `relocation_research.md` per geography; (5) role scope — HFT-critical-path proximity vs adjacent. Add a tie-breaker rule. (~30 min)
  - 🎯 Hardened acceptance: the criteria block lists all five dimensions, EACH with an explicit weight (or ranked order) AND a concrete pass-bar/threshold (e.g. comp floor as a number, "fully-remote = hard requirement"); the visa dimension cites `relocation_research.md`; a written tie-breaker rule exists. A criterion with no threshold is a vibe, not a criterion — reject and tighten.
  - *Mental model*: offer day floods you with dopamine and time pressure, the two worst conditions for a high-stakes decision. Pre-committing the rubric while you are calm is a precommitment device — it lets sober-you bind offer-day-you, so the W131 decision is computed against weights you set on reflection, not improvised under a recruiter's "we need an answer by Friday." This is the single artifact that most changes the W131 outcome.
  - Output: `progress.md` contains the `Offer decision criteria` block with all five weighted+thresholded dimensions + tie-breaker; `grep -A30 'Offer decision criteria' progress.md` shows every dimension carries a number or hard/soft tag.
