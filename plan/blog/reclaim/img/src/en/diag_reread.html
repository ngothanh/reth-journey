Announce, then re-read: if the root still holds A, the scan must see the slot
T1 — readerT2 — popper / scheme
1reads head: gets A·
2writes A into its slot·
3reads head again: still A → opens A·
4·swings head from A to B
5·retires A
6·scans: finds A in T1's slot
7·does not free A — T1 is safe
The scheme only receives A after head has left it, so a re-read that still sees A proves the slot was written before any scan. (This holds only with a full fence between the slot write and the re-read.)
