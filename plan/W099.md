  - *Back-of-envelope* (bar (c) — name the cost):
    - **Full-recompute cliff**: 100k accounts × 10 ticks/s = **1M recomputes/s**. At ~1µs/account = **1 CPU-second/second single-threaded** — the whole core, gone, before a second instrument. Shard-by-account (16 shards) → ~62.5k/s/shard ≈ 62ms/s/shard, comfortable — *but only if each tick recomputes the whole shard*; a per-trade mark cadence (≫10/s) blows even that. **Incremental** drops steady state to O(touched): a single mark tick on BTC-PERP touches only accounts holding BTC-PERP, deltas each ≈ ~50ns → ~tens of µs/tick at realistic position counts. Cliff ≈ full-recompute ÷ incremental ≈ 16× at the target, unbounded at higher cadence.
    - **Alloc-on-tick cliff**: malloc ≈ 50ns. A `Vec<AccountId>` dirty-set re-allocated per tick at 10 ticks/s × (re-grow) + a `Vec<Position>` per `AccountMargin` clone ≈ allocs that pollute cache and jitter p99.99. Fixed-cap dirty-set + `Copy` `AccountMargin` = 0 allocs.
    - **Read-contract cliff**: a VSR read to quorum ≈ 1 RTT ≈ 250µs loopback. A margin check per mark tick that pays a consensus round-trip = 250µs *floor* on every check — impossible at 10 ticks/s × 100k accounts. **Local applied state on the leader shard** = an in-memory read of the engine's own snapshot of the VSR-applied log ≈ tens of ns. Cliff ≈ 1000×+.

  - **The problem this primitive solves**: a portfolio-margin engine computed naïvely (full recompute per tick + a consensus read per margin check + `f64` PnL) (a) does not fit the budget at 100k accounts, (b) serializes every margin check on an RTT, and (c) diverges across replicas — defeating the W108 cluster-VOPR convergence + tripwire-never-hit invariant. It also tempts **scope creep**: pulling rolling empirical correlation in "for accuracy" injects non-deterministic float state the VSR cluster would have to replicate bit-identically. The fix: incremental delta updates + local-applied-state reads + integer-only deterministic netting with the const 30% haircut, and a hard v1.5 scope gate.

## Tuesday — `risk-engine`: VOPR over the margin core (determinism under random schedules)

- [ ] [HFT] **Build (bar (c))**: `crates/risk-engine/src/vopr.rs` — a VOPR-style deterministic simulator over the margin core: a single-PRNG-seeded op generator emits random sequences of `{MarkTick, PositionChange, Snapshot, FullRecompute(CB)}`, applies them across 3 in-process replicas, and a StateChecker asserts replica convergence at every commit point. (~total 170 min across 3 phases)
  - 🔧 **Why this matters**: the deterministic-core discipline (§3 row 21) is the cross-cutting invariant the whole capstone rests on — *margin/liquidation/settlement must replay bit-identically across replicas*. VOPR (TigerBeetle's pattern) is how you *prove* it before W108's cluster-VOPR extends it to the whole venue.
